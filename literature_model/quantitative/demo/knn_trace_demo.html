How the model designs a flowsheet

For a real deposit, the synthesizer finds its nearest analogs among disclosed operations and lets them vote on every process block and connection. Below is the flowsheet it produced — and, underneath, the analog projects and votes that built it. Nothing is re-scored; this is the model's own arithmetic, drawn.
